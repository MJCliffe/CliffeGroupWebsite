Accelerated Aging for Making Porous MOFs

Figure: An example of a typical aging reaction on a 10 g scale, with a Canadian penny for size comparison.

In our previous work, we showed that accelerated aging is a practical method for making metal-organic frameworks (MOFs) from cheap and safe starting materials without needing bulk solvent or high temperatures. In this paper we demonstrate that it is possible to directly make porous MOFs using accelerated aging. We also extend the chemistry to additional metals.
