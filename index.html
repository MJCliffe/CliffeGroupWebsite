FIHM Group

We are the Functional Inorganic and Hybrid Materials (FIHM) Group, based in the Department of Materials Science and Metallurgy at the University of Cambridge.

Our research focuses on exploring metal-organic frameworks (MOFs) as quantum materials. We are interested in how we can design MOFs with new and unusual magnetic and electronic behaviour.

Our work is generously supported by the UKRI Frontiers ERC Guarantee Funding Project ‘DISCO-MOF’.

If you are interested in joining us, whether as a project student, graduate student or postdoctoral fellow, please get in touch with a CV to explore possible funding options. We welcome dynamic and creative scientists regardless of ethnicity, gender, gender identity or sexual orientation.
